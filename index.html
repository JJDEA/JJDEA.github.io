What it is

Calendar Widget is a small personal desktop app for Windows. It shows upcoming events from one or more Google accounts on the desktop, in a frosted-glass panel that takes its colours from the wallpaper. It starts automatically when Windows starts.

Features

  • Agenda of upcoming events and a month grid, merged from several Google accounts
  • Create, edit and delete events, and join video calls
  • Windows notifications before events start
  • Themes: blurred wallpaper, see-through or solid, with custom colours, fonts and presets

How it uses Google data

The app talks directly to Google Calendar from your own computer. There is no server, and none of your calendar data is sent to the developer or anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for details.
